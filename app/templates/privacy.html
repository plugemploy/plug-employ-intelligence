Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Plug Employ Intelligence ("Plug Employ", "we") helps businesses that sell local advertising find and follow up with local businesses. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your Google sign-in: your name and email address. We never see your Google password.
  • What you tell us: your company name, a description of what you sell, your ZIP codes and the kinds of businesses you want to reach.
  • Your work in the app: the leads you track, their statuses, notes, follow-up dates, sales you log, and feedback or survey answers you send us.
  • Gmail, only if you connect it: see the Gmail section below.
  • Close CRM, only if you connect it: your Close API key, stored encrypted, used to send leads to Close and read their status back.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach our servers. We keep only Stripe's customer and subscription reference numbers and your subscription status.
  • Cookies: a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in, plus small preference cookies (such as your lead list filters). We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Business information we gather for you

Leads come from Overture Maps Foundation's open places data, which is published under open licenses. To score leads, we read the public web pages of those businesses (respecting each site's robots.txt). This is public information about businesses, not about you.

Gmail

If you choose to connect Gmail, we use Google's official Gmail API to:

  • check whether you've already emailed a lead's business, using only the date of the latest email to or from that business's web address. We don't read or store the text of your emails.
  • save email drafts in your Gmail for you to review and send yourself, and send your weekly summary to you. We never send email to your leads.

Your Gmail connection is stored encrypted. You can disconnect it at any time on the My business page, or from your Google account's security settings. Plug Employ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI models, and never shared except as described in this policy.

How we use AI

We use Anthropic's Claude to score leads, suggest kinds of businesses and write email drafts. To do that, we send Claude your business description and public information about the lead businesses. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers that run the app for us: Render (hosting and database), Google (sign-in and Gmail), Anthropic (AI scoring), Stripe (payments) and, if you connect it, Close. Each uses your data only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. Ask us to delete your account and we'll delete your account data within 30 days, except what we must keep for tax or legal reasons (such as payment records).

Security

The app runs over encrypted connections (https), and connection keys such as Gmail and Close are stored encrypted.
