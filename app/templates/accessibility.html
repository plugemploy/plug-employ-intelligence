Accessibility

Last updated November 6, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use Plug Employ Intelligence, including people who use a keyboard instead of a mouse, a screen reader, zoom, or other assistive technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we've done

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a visible outline showing where you are, and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Form fields and buttons have names a screen reader can read out.
  • Pop-ups take keyboard focus and close with the Escape key.
  • Progress updates (like a search finding leads) are announced to screen readers.
  • Text colors meet contrast guidelines, and links inside sentences are underlined.
  • Animations stop if your device is set to reduce motion. A night mode is in the top bar.
  • We check our pages with automated accessibility tests.

Known limits

The Google sign-in, Stripe payment and Close CRM pages are run by those companies. Lead information comes from public business data and businesses' own websites, which we don't control.
